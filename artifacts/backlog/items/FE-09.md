## What

Three designs — the agent contract, the ledger mapping and significance boundary, and the Standard 17 record — written without modifying the specimen.

## Why

Designing against a specimen you are also editing makes it impossible to tell which observations were caused by the observer.

## Notes

Read-only was not a courtesy. It is what let the specimen stay a specimen.
