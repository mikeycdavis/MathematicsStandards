## What

Represent the warrant behind an attestation: who or what reviewed, and with what independence.

## Why

Divergent and therefore the strongest evidence of a real gap: two careful adopters met it and resolved it in opposite directions — fourteen attestations under an AI-authorship disclosure in an unread comment, versus zero on the ground that an agent's scan is not a human's warrant.

## Notes

Deferred deliberately. 14 versus 0 is not noise to reconcile; it proves underdetermination, not which interpretation is correct. Choosing now, on no evidence, is exactly how the first-unproved-bridge error would have happened.
