## What

parseObligation returns a third state, unrecognised, instead of silently defaulting unreadable lines to open; the detector splits into a blocking OBSERVED arm and a capping UNKNOWN arm.

## Why

Writing more explanation in an Obligations field than the positional grammar covered produced a verdict nothing can clear, about a proposition nobody established.

## Notes

UNKNOWN rather than INFERRED, deliberately: INFERRED would claim approximate grounds for believing the obligation is open, and there are none — the line could not be read. This is the framework's first emission of UNKNOWN and the reason the label vocabulary has four entries rather than two. capsSupport was widened to `state !== discharged` so an unreadable obligation still caps what rests on it; narrowing it would have traded the false verdict for a false negative.
