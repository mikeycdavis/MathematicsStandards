## What

Introduce a data-sufficiency state so a rule that found nothing to examine is distinguishable in the output from one that examined everything and was satisfied.

## Why

Replicated. RH: 23 rules passed with no ledger in existence, and the answer got less true once a ledger appeared. PvsNP: literature.resolvable-identifiers passed having examined nothing — 16 references, not one DOI or arXiv id — reported identically to a real check.

## Notes

Detector availability and data sufficiency are different things and v1.0 has one output for both. Related to §0h but not a duplicate: this one needs a state, that one needs a subject.
