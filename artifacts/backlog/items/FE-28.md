## What

A representation for checks that apply to a research attempt rather than to a claim.

## Why

Replicated in role: RH's gates G1-G5 and PvsNP's per-iteration relativization, natural-proofs, algebrization and literature checks. Neither has anywhere to live in v1.0.

## Notes

The role replicates; the content does not replicate at all, and a framework that fixed the content would be wrong for both.
