## What

Migrate eleven claims, run first with zero attestations and then with fourteen, and measure the difference.

## Why

Whether attestations get written at all was the single largest open question about the mechanism.

## Notes

The trial produced the framework's two genuine findings — both from manual review, neither from a detector. It also produced a rejected attestation that is still standing, and an uncorrected overclaim in CLM-0008 that was deliberately not fixed: correcting it immediately would collapse detection and remediation into the same historical state.
