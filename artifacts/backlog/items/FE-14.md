## What

Carry the evidence label into the result, remove report()'s default entirely, and classify every call site by hand.

## Why

The ceiling is worthless if a heuristic can still call itself OBSERVED.

## Notes

Not 'the default becomes INFERRED' — no default at all. Flipping a default moves the hidden assumption in the opposite direction and leaves the mechanism intact: the next detector still gets its epistemic status decided by a function signature written months earlier.
