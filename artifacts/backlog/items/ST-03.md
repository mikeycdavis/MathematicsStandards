## What

Verify the adapter declaration against an independent oracle from a fresh extraction rather than from the working tree.

## Why

A contract test that reads the same working tree it describes can agree with itself and still be wrong.

## Notes

This is the check that exposed the invocation test writing scaffolding into this repository — a mutating test with no disposable target.
