## What

Add detectors so that more of the 82 rules are machine-backed.

## Why

53 of 82 is not an optimization target, and both trials strengthened the case rather than weakening it: the framework's two genuine findings both came from manual judgement, and its most serious failures both came from automated detectors.

## Notes

A project's assurance and the tooling's maturity do not combine into one number, and moving the second would not have found either defect.
