## What

The vendored YAML reader rejects block scalars, so an attestation's entire substance must be one physical line with no double quotes.

## Why

Replicated, and it bit harder in the second project: a policy using `>-` for a multi-line reason was rejected outright — NOT_EVALUATED, exit 2 — and every value in that project's policy is now a single long quoted line with a comment explaining why.

## Notes

The strictness is right and should stay: a reader that quietly ignores a construct is worse than one that refuses it. The defect is that a mechanism intended to carry expert reasoning has a format that punishes reasoning at length, and neither the template nor INSTRUCTIONS.md warns about it. Fourteen attestations averaging roughly 700 characters were folded onto single lines and had their quotation marks stripped.
