## What

Make weak or misparsed evidence incapable of producing an unjustifiably absolute invariant verdict. Covers §0b and §0i as one milestone.

## Why

Both are invariants, so both stop work, and neither can be cleared without violating Standard 21. This is the framework producing precisely the failure it exists to prevent: epistemic strength increasing somewhere between evidence collection and conclusion.

## Notes

Implemented on branch tier1-verdict-strength in a separate worktree at F:/Repos/MathematicsStandards.tier1; not yet merged to main, which is why this epic is IN_REVIEW rather than COMPLETE. §0c was deliberately excluded — the engine should stop treating weakly supported findings as certainty before a detector is asked to carry the epistemic safety of the whole system.
