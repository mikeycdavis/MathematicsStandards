## What

Stop rules reading a present, correct representation of required evidence as its absence.

## Why

Replicated. RH: an `O(` marks a statement approximate when the O is the bound. PvsNP: `\babout\b` matched an English preposition, and the suppressor missed `bounds` against `\bbound\b`.

## Notes

Deliberately excluded from Tier 1: the engine should stop treating weakly supported findings as certainty before a detector is asked to carry the epistemic safety of the whole system. Adjacent to §0d — a shared evidence-recognition model would address both.
