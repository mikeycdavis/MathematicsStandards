## What

Reconcile the evidence-type vocabulary across rules that disagree about what a type is worth.

## Why

`citation` is adequate support in PROOF_EVIDENCE and absent from the counterexample skip list, so a published 2005 theorem was asked to search for counterexamples.

## Notes

Single adopter, RH.

**Why eight sites moved when the field trial named one disagreement.** This is acceptance-scope clarification found by the falsifier, not a new feature and not scope creep. The recorded defect was one drifted pair: `citation` in `PROOF_EVIDENCE` and absent from `detectCounterexampleSearch`'s inline list. The C4 contract test — written to state the guarantee rather than the instance — found eight literal evidence-type tokens across four detectors. Only one pair was known to disagree; the rest were private copies of the same declaration that happened to agree, which is exactly the state the counterexample list was in for `computational` until something asked. Narrowing the item back to "make these two consumers agree" after that would have been designing to the observed instance instead of to the guarantee this item states.

Three of the eight turned out not to be evidence-type lists at all: the ledger field named `formal`, and the applicability regimes `computational` and `formal`. Three vocabularies, one spelling. The falsifier had recognised a token without the context that says which vocabulary it belongs to — which is §0c, committed by the test written to prove §0d, and it is recorded in the design rather than quietly corrected. Five sites were genuine and all five are migrated.

Implemented on branch tier3-semantic-consistency, unmerged. Evidence types now carry declared capabilities; `PROOF_EVIDENCE` is a view over that declaration rather than a second source; detectors keep their own domain questions and ask the declaration what a type is worth. The contract test is retained and mutation-checked: reintroducing one private list turns it red.
