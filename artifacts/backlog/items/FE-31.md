## What

Reject an unrecognised or malformed argument before the command mutates anything, and prove it for every command rather than for init alone.

## Why

Replicated, and elevated on safety: RH had `init --help` apply against the repository, and PvsNP had `init <dir> --dryrun` write all seven paths and exit 0. An unrecognised flag causing a destructive mutation is not an ergonomics defect.

## Notes

Complete and measured on branch fix-0a-fail-closed — 92 tests green, up from 79, and both specimens re-run byte-identical, which was the acceptance condition. Held at IN_REVIEW rather than COMPLETE because it is not merged to main; FE-16 tracks landing it.
