## What

Require every research programme to identify the earliest unestablished link in its dependency structure.

## Why

Falsified by the second adopter. RH's contracts C1-C8 are chains, so each has an earliest unestablished link. PvsNP has no equivalent concept and dropped a sequential ordering rule on its own record as wrong: its lower-bound tracks import only the clause and counting layers, use no Turing machine, and serialising them behind Cook-Levin would have bought nothing and cost the two closures that came out of running them.

## Notes

Cardinality and order are properties of the research programme, and the framework does not get to assume either. RH's frontier is a point because its dependency structure is a chain; PvsNP's is an antichain. Mandating this would have pushed a project back toward a serialisation it had already identified and corrected — the exact 'more machine-legible at the expense of the research model' failure the experiment exists to detect. This was the strongest-looking candidate either adoption produced, and it is the argument for the two-producer rule itself. What survives is narrower and deferred: A2.
