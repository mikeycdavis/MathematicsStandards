## What

Select which declarations belong in a ledger using prose-reference, exported-versus-internal, or dependency-reachability.

## Why

Three of the four candidate signals are falsified by measurement. 781 of 987 declarations are named somewhere in prose, but 799 of 970 appear only in log documents and the three most important synthesis documents name zero — so the signal selects nearly everything and its negation selects the wrong things. Exported-versus-internal fails identically in a library organised by namespace. Dependency-reachability inherits the chain assumption rejected in FE-34.

## Notes

Do not build these three.
