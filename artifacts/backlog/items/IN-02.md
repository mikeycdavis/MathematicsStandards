## What

Adopt the frozen v1.0.0 framework against two independent mathematical research projects, record what happens without repairing the framework mid-run, and dispose of the resulting evidence.

## Why

Answers the governing question: does the framework add assurance to an already rigorous project without pushing that project to become less truthful, more duplicative, or more machine-legible at the expense of its research model?

## Notes

The experimental discipline was absolute: record rather than fix, never modify a specimen to make the framework happier, never optimize a number, stop at every boundary for authorization. Both trials ran against the immutable v1.0.0 tag at dc29992, extracted with git archive — not against the repository tip, which had already moved to 1.0.1.
