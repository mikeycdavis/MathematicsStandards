## What

A policy-as-code standards framework for mathematical research whose own reports never claim more confidence than the evidence behind them supports.

## Why

The pack exists to stop exploration, computation, reformulation, and plausibility being mistaken for proof. A framework that itself overstates what it checked would be committing the error it was built to prevent.

## Notes

This theme is the standing measure for every item below. Two field trials showed the framework failing it in both directions: reporting passes for rules that examined nothing, and reporting unclearable blocks from regex matches. Both are the same defect wearing opposite signs.
