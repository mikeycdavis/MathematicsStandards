## What

Ten rule files, the project-policy schema with its mathematics block, and the framework's own policy classifying every research rule not-applicable with a reason and a revisitWhen.

## Why

Catalog owns identity, policy owns applicability, evaluator owns evidence — and none may redefine the others.

## Notes

Schema and the repository's real policy were authored in the same sitting deliberately. Writing a real policy is what reveals that 'this does not apply to us' and 'this applies and we are knowingly failing it' must not share a mechanism.
