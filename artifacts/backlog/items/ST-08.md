## What

Decide which of 987 declarations belong in a claims ledger and on what signal.

## Why

Registering everything would have made the ledger a second copy of the source tree.

## Notes

This is where the four candidate significance signals were written down. Three of them were later falsified by measurement: 781 of 987 declarations are named in prose, but the three most important synthesis documents name none of them, so the signal selects nearly everything and its negation selects the wrong things.
