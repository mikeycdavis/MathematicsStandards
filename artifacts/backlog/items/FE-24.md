## What

A representation for a machine-settleable check that bears on a manual rule without discharging it.

## Why

Confirmed in both adopters. What is missing is a model, not confirmation.

## Notes

Deferred with §0l — they are one design question approached from the evidence side and the reviewer side. v1.0 has one slot for non-automated evidence and at least three things need it: human expert review, an agent's mechanical check, and an agent's judgement.
