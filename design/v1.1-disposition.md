# v1.1 evidence disposition

**What this document is, and what it is not.** Two independent field trials of v1.0.0 are complete
and preserved. This assigns every candidate a disposition against that evidence, so that
[`v1.1-candidates.md`](v1.1-candidates.md) records *observations* and this records *decisions*.

It is **not** a v1.1 design and not an implementation plan. Nothing here is built. An `ACCEPT FOR
v1.1 DESIGN` means the evidence justifies designing a change, not that the change is known.

**Why it exists.** A candidate file that only ever grows becomes a backlog, and a backlog is a
document in which nothing is ever rejected. Two candidates are rejected below on evidence, and one of
them is an abstraction that looked like the most promising thing either adoption produced.

## 1. Standing: replicated versus single-adopter

These are not the same kind of evidence and must not receive the same design priority merely because
they appear in the same file.

**Replicated** — observed failing in both projects, on different subject matter, by agents who had
not seen each other's findings. A property of the framework.

> §0 · §0a · §0b · §0c · §0g · §0m

**Single-adopter** — real, preserved, reproducible, and *not yet known to generalize*. Found later,
which is a fact about sequence and not about weight.

> §0d · §0e · §0f · §0h · §0i · §0j · §0k

**Divergent** — both adopters met it and resolved it in opposite directions. Strongest possible
evidence that the gap is real; no evidence at all about which resolution is right.

> §0l

A single-adopter finding may still outrank a replicated one on severity — §0i does. The two axes are
independent, and the priority tiers in §3 are ordered by severity, with standing noted beside each.

### REJECT / FALSIFIED

| Candidate | Why it is rejected |
| --- | --- |
| **First unproved bridge, as a general requirement** | **Falsified by the second adopter.** RH's contracts C1–C8 are chains, so each has an earliest unestablished link. PvsNP has no equivalent concept and dropped a sequential ordering rule *on the record* as wrong — its lower-bound tracks import only the clause and counting layers, use no Turing machine, and serialising them behind Cook–Levin "would have bought nothing and cost the two closures that came out of running them." RH's frontier is a point because its dependency structure is a chain; PvsNP's is an antichain. **Cardinality and order are properties of the research programme, and the framework does not get to assume either.** Mandating it would have pushed a project back toward a serialisation it had already identified and corrected — the exact "more machine-legible at the expense of the research model" failure the experiment exists to detect. |
| **Ledger-reconciliation signals: prose-referenced, exported-vs-internal, dependency-reachable** | Three of the four candidate significance signals in [§1 of the candidates file](v1.1-candidates.md#1-ledger-reconciliation-against-a-formal-development) are falsified by measurement. RH: 781 of 987 declarations are named somewhere in prose, but 799 of 970 appear only in log documents, and the three most important synthesis documents name **zero** — so the signal selects nearly everything and its negation selects the wrong things. Exported-vs-internal fails identically in a library organised by namespace. Dependency-reachability inherits the chain assumption rejected above. **Do not build these three.** |

Both rejections are recorded in the candidates file at their entries. Neither is deleted — a
falsified candidate is evidence, and removing it would let it be re-proposed.

## 3. Priority, ordered by assurance corruption

Not by ergonomics, not by coverage, not by how cheap the fix looks.

### Tier 1 — false invariant blocks

> **§0b** (replicated) · **§0i** (single, PvsNP)

Weak or misparsed evidence becomes an absolute conclusion. Both are invariants, so both stop work,
and neither can be cleared without a Standard 21 violation. This is the framework producing precisely
the failure it exists to prevent: **epistemic strength increasing somewhere between evidence
collection and conclusion.**

Both adopters left these blocks standing rather than clear them, which is the correct behaviour and
is also why they are top priority — correct behaviour under a defective verdict is not a stable state
to leave adopters in.

### Tier 2 — false assurance

> **§0** (replicated) · **§0e** (single) · **§0h** (single) · **§0m** (replicated)

The framework reports confidence it has not established, or allows established assurance to decay
silently. §0 and §0h report passes that examined nothing or examined the wrong project. §0m reports a
pass on the framework's own thin artifact while blind to the strong native one. §0e lets a real human
judgement remain indistinguishable from a current one indefinitely.

§0e belongs here rather than with the authoring defects: a formatting burden is a cost, but an
assurance that silently stops being true is a defect of the same kind as Tier 1, one step further from
the verdict.

### Tier 3 — semantic inconsistency and representation

> **§0c** (replicated) · **§0d** (single) · **§0j** (deferred) · **§0k** (deferred) · **§0l**
> (divergent) · **A1** (replicated, accepted)

The framework's model disagrees with the mathematics, with itself, or cannot hold something two
projects need. These mislead, but they do not manufacture certainty. A1 sits here because adding an
axis is a representation change, not a corruption fix.

### Tier 4 — authoring and ergonomics

> **§0f** (single, observed) · **§0g** (replicated)

Real, measured, and they cost adopters real effort. They do not corrupt an assurance claim.

> **§0a** — ergonomic in shape, **elevated on safety.** An unrecognised flag causing a *destructive
> mutation* is not an ergonomics defect. It has the lowest assurance impact and a high blast radius,
> and it is the cheapest item in this document to fix correctly.

## 4. What remains explicitly not planned

Reaffirmed, now with two trials behind each.

- **More detectors to raise `frameworkCoverage`.** 53/82 is not an optimization target, and the trials
  strengthened the case rather than weakening it: **the framework's two genuine findings both came
  from manual judgement, and its most serious failures both came from automated detectors.** A
  project's assurance and the tooling's maturity do not combine, and moving the second number would
  not have found either defect.
- **More standards.** 22 cover the source and the charter. Neither adopter asked for a 23rd; both
  asked for existing rules to work.
- **Network-backed citation checking.** RH's two citation rules were the ones it *could not* review —
  the obstacle is having the papers, and both adopters recorded declining rather than guessing.
- **A profile mechanism.** Applicability already tailors per rule; §0k asks for a third *value*, not a
  named bundle that hides which rules it turned off.

The mechanism held under the strongest available pressure |
