## What

A design pinning the acceptance rule, the verdict ceiling, the predicted effect on both frozen specimens, and four mutations — all before compliance.mjs was touched.

## Why

Define how a proposed guarantee can be falsified before implementing the mechanism intended to guarantee it. That discipline is what exposed the v1.0 failures in the first place.

## Notes

The design predicts the specimens' post-change behaviour in writing, so the implementation can be judged against the predictions rather than described by them. It also revised one prediction before the run — two of RH's three blockers are rejected attestations carrying no evidence label at all, so the cap had to key on an explicit non-elevating label rather than on `label !== OBSERVED`.

The design document itself is `design/v1.1-tier1-verdict-strength.md`, which exists only on `tier1-verdict-strength` — it is not reachable from `main`, so the evidence here is commit shas rather than a path. That resolves once FE-16 lands.
