## What

Make an attestation's staleness visible: the schema says the validator prints the digest and it does not.

## Why

Single adopter, and serious out of proportion to its size. Staleness is what makes a historical human judgement usable as current assurance.

## Notes

Not printed in the render, the summary, or the JSON, for any of fourteen attestations. An assurance that silently stops being true is a defect of the same kind as Tier 1, one step further from the verdict.
