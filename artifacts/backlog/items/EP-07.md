## Why this stays open after FE-42, 2026-08-26

FE-42 closed on 2026-08-26 and was this epic's last open child, so the validator would now accept
`COMPLETE`. It is deliberately not taken, for two reasons that are about this epic's subject rather
than about its child list.

**The behaviour is unreleased.** This epic's own precedent is that it closes after a release, not
after a landing: the 2026-08-12 closure names `29dde68` for the landing *and* `3bc38fa` for the 1.2.0
release, and FE-41 held at `IN_REVIEW` from `912d4f0` until `v3.0.0` for the same reason. FE-42's
repair is on `main` at `00c8e3f` with no version assigned — correctly, because the release contract
does not require one — but "no release is required" is not "the epic's work has been published under
a number", and closing here would be the first time this epic treated those as the same fact.

**One false-assurance question inside this epic's subject is open and unowned.** FE-42's design check
established, red on `main@8e77453`, that a ledger citing `proofs/clm-0002.md#no-such-section` passes
`evidence.artifact-linked` and is counted in `inspected.surfaces` as a resolved artifact — a surface
reporting resolution it did not achieve, which is this epic's definition of the defect. It was left
unfixed on purpose: the catalog has promised anchor-stripping since `168fcd3`, so changing it amends
a published rule description and needs its own disposition. That is a scoping decision about FE-42,
not a finding that the question is settled.

It is also the unfinished remainder of **§0m** — [FE-20](FE-20.md), *a rule that accepts a pointer
must be able to follow it*. `scripts/pointers.mjs:97-102` refuses to truncate a fragment and says
why; the four ledger consumers truncate and pass. Two surfaces of one repository disagree about one
locator, and only one of them is on the path adopters' ledgers travel.

**No item has been filed for it.** That is a `backlog` decision, and closing this epic before it is
made would bury the finding in a closed parent rather than leave it visible. Whether it becomes a
child here, a child of EP-08, or a rule-change item with its own release boundary is open.
