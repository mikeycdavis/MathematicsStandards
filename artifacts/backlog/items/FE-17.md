## What

Introduce a data-sufficiency state so a rule that found nothing to examine is distinguishable in the output from one that examined everything and was satisfied.

## Why

Replicated. RH: 23 rules passed with no ledger in existence, and the answer got less true once a ledger appeared. PvsNP: literature.resolvable-identifiers passed having examined nothing — 16 references, not one DOI or arXiv id — reported identically to a real check.

## Notes

Detector availability and data sufficiency are different things and v1.0 has one output for both. Related to §0h but not a duplicate: this one needs a state, that one needs a subject.

Implemented on branch tier2-evidence-provenance, unmerged, and recorded here from the branch rather than from a plan — the seeding on 2026-08-11 read the disposition and the merged history and did not enumerate the worktrees. The mechanism is an `inspected` field on every result carrying a subject, a derived tri-state and the resolved surfaces; `no-subject` makes the rule `skipped / not-evaluated` with a reason that distinguishes it from "no detector exists". Full acceptance chain: guarantee C1 stated before implementation, four red tests, the substrate, and both frozen adopters measured. Held at IN_REVIEW because none of it is reachable from main.
