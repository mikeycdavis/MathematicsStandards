## What

computation.scope-declared requires a range and an arithmetic; CLM-0010 is a certified interval-arithmetic bound at a single parameter value, deliberately.

## Why

Observed, not yet a defect. The claim's Quantifiers field says so and the detector does not read it.

## Notes

One claim is not enough to say whether the model needs a `scope: point` shape or should read Quantifiers. The second adopter did not recur it, so it stays deferred rather than becoming design work.
