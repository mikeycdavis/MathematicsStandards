## What

Build the standalone framework: 22 standards, a rule catalog, a zero-dependency toolchain, tests, CI, and an invocation contract.

## Why

The original prompt and the standards-repository charter both ask for a structured, auditable, testable system rather than a document of best practice.

## Notes

Built in the order the framework's own author recommended: source spec and the inventory/fidelity gates operational before a single standard document was written.
